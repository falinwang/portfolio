2019 MassChallenge 實習日記分析 MassChallenge 2019 Internship Diary Analysis

把舊日記挖成新故事

這份互動報告把 2019 年 MassChallenge 實習日記拆解成可用於作品集、面試與自我定位的故事資產。重點不是懷舊，而是判斷：哪些經驗到了 2026 年仍然有價值，哪些需要重新包裝，哪些不能過度聲稱。 This interactive report turns the 2019 MassChallenge internship diaries into story assets for portfolio, interviews, and positioning. The question is not nostalgia; it is what still matters in 2026, what needs reframing, and what should not be overstated.

核心判斷

這批日記真正有價值的地方，不是「我做了校友平台改版」，而是你當時已經在做問題重構、資料品質判斷、跨利害關係人對齊，以及資源配對系統的早期產品思考。 The value is not simply "I redesigned an alumni platform." The stronger story is problem reframing, data-quality judgment, stakeholder alignment, and early product thinking around resource matching.

最強主線

從參與度問題改寫成價值問題 From engagement to value problem

日記反覆顯示：低使用率不是介面摩擦，而是平台只向校友索取資料，卻沒有給回足夠價值。這是最適合放進作品集和產品經理、產品設計面試的故事。 The diaries repeatedly show that low usage was not just interface friction. The platform asked alumni for data without giving enough value back.

2026 轉譯

資源匹配不是過時平台題

以今天的語言來看，這是生命週期產品、知識圖譜、分類語彙、推薦體驗、客戶關係資料品質的交界題。不要硬說成 AI 專案，但可以說它是 AI 時代配對系統的前身。 In today's language, this sits between lifecycle product, knowledge graph, taxonomy, recommendation UX, and CRM data quality. It is not an AI project, but it is relevant to AI-era matching systems.

最大提醒

影響數字要分層說

既有草稿提到約 50 萬美元的平台退場。日記能支撐「為什麼 Mobilize 不值得繼續」的因果鏈，但公開使用前要再查證精確合約金額、決策負責人與時間點。 The existing draft mentions an approximately $500K platform sunset. The diaries support the causal logic, but the exact contract value, decision owner, and timeline should be verified before public use.

這條時間軸可以幫你在面試時講清楚「我不是一開始就知道答案，而是怎麼逐步改變策略」。 This timeline helps you explain that the answer did not appear on day one; the strategy changed as evidence accumulated.

6/17

進入環境，建立人際地圖

第一天記下入職引導、設計主管、同事，以及資料、成長、工程等角色。這是後續跨部門研究的起點。 Day one captured onboarding and the early map of design, data, growth, and engineering stakeholders.

6/19

研究問題被定義出來

開始準備利害關係人訪談問題，關鍵問題已經包含校友價值、回訪使用者、Mobilize 平台，以及既有資料如何被蒐集。 The stakeholder questions already included alumni value, returning users, Mobilize, and how data was captured.

6/21

北極星指標、推薦值與校友系統研究 North Star Metric, NPS, and alumni-system study

你開始把參與度往「持續提供價值」與「領先指標」推進，這讓後來的問題重構有理論支架。 Engagement started to become a question of continuous value and leading indicators.

7/01

關鍵問題重構成形 Key reframe takes shape

利害關係人整理裡已經出現資源配對、合作夥伴入口、校友機會、公司資料不準，以及「低參與是因為沒有價值」這幾個核心訊號。 The synthesis surfaced matching, partner portal, alumni opportunities, weak startup profiles, and low engagement caused by weak value.

7/08

招募受訪者卡住，開始調整話術

你重寫追蹤信，從單純的「訪談邀請」改成「了解加速器結束後的挑戰，以及什麼支援真的有用」。 The follow-up email shifted from a generic interview request to learning about post-accelerator challenges and useful support.

7/18

研究方法從正式招募轉為機會式訪談 From formal recruiting to opportunistic interviewing

設計主管建議看到駐點校友就直接抓 5 到 20 分鐘訪談。這是很好的實戰研究故事：在限制下換方法，而不是等完美條件。 The research approach changed to short, opportunistic interviews with Alumni-in-Residence when formal recruiting stalled.

7/29

以色列專案訪談打開組織策略層 Israel-program interview opens the organizational layer

以色列專案的訪談把問題推到全組織目標、分類語彙錯位、Mobilize 採用率，以及為什麼要維持多年校友關係。 The interview reframed the issue around organization-wide goals, taxonomy mismatch, Mobilize adoption, and long-term alumni relationships.

8/01

親和圖整理與原型發想 Affinity synthesis and prototype brainstorming

日記顯示你進入親和圖筆記、人物誌、實習成果展示，以及與主管和產品夥伴對齊洞察的階段。故事從研究轉進設計決策。 The work moved from affinity notes and personas into prototype direction, showcase preparation, and stakeholder alignment.

2026 還有什麼價值？

答案是：有，而且比「2019 的 UI」更有價值。價值主要在問題定義與系統思考，而不是視覺稿本身。 Yes, it still has value in 2026. The strongest value is problem definition and systems thinking, not the 2019 interface visuals themselves.

高價值

產品策略 Product Strategy

從利害關係人的需求裡抽出真正的商業問題，能證明你不是只接需求做畫面，而是會問「這個功能是否該存在」。 Extracting the real business problem from stakeholder asks shows that you can question whether a feature should exist at all.

高價值

資料品質與分類語彙 Data Quality / Taxonomy

新創公司的自我描述與組織分類不一致，是今天客戶關係管理、AI 搜尋、推薦系統、知識圖譜都會遇到的硬問題。 The mismatch between startup self-description and organizational taxonomy is central to CRM, AI search, recommendation systems, and knowledge graphs.

中高價值

限制下的研究執行 Research Ops Under Constraint

受訪者難找、時間短、資料分散，你仍改變招募方法、用既有資料加速決策。這比「完美流程」更可信。 Recruiting was difficult, time was short, and data was scattered. Changing methods under constraint is more credible than showing a perfect process.

需重包裝

介面畫面 UI Screens

2019 年的視覺和互動樣式可能老了，但可作為決策證物。展示時要讓畫面服務於故事，而不是只展示畫面。 The 2019 screens may look dated, but they still work as decision artifacts if the visuals support the story.

面試與作品集用法

最推薦把它包成「我如何在低明確度、高限制、跨部門資料混亂的情境下，重新定義問題並推動設計方向」。 The strongest framing: how you redefined the problem and moved the design direction forward under ambiguity, constraints, and messy cross-functional data.

30 秒版本

「MassChallenge 原本要我提升校友參與度，但研究後我發現校友不是不會用平台，而是平台沒有提供回報價值。我把問題改寫成『持續提供價值』，設計以新創目標驅動的資源配對流程，同時處理公司分類語彙不準造成的配對問題。」 MassChallenge originally asked me to improve alumni engagement. Through research, I found that alumni did not avoid the platform because they could not use it; they avoided it because it did not return value. I reframed the work around continued value delivery, designed a goal-driven resource-matching flow, and addressed the taxonomy problem behind poor matching.

情境任務行動結果結構 STAR Structure

  • 情境：2,000 多名校友、Mobilize 採用率低、40% 個人資料不準、利害關係人對目標不一致。Situation: 2,000+ alumni, low Mobilize adoption, 40% inaccurate profiles, and misaligned stakeholder goals.
  • 任務：在 10 週內找出為什麼校友不回來，並提出可落地的產品方向。Task: Find why alumni were not returning and propose a feasible product direction within ten weeks.
  • 行動：進行利害關係人訪談、競品分析、既有問卷與資料整理、校友訪談、親和圖分析、影響力與投入成本排序。Action: Stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, existing survey/data synthesis, alumni interviews, affinity mapping, and impact-effort prioritization.
  • 結果：將任務簡報從「提升參與度」改成「持續提供價值」；提出以目標驅動的資源配對，以及自由填寫公司專長的資料輸入方式。50 萬美元影響需查證後再公開聲稱。Result: Reframed the brief from engagement to continued value, proposed goal-driven resource matching and free-form specialty input. The $500K impact should be verified before public claims.

公開前風險

這批日記很有料，但也有內部連結、姓名、電子郵件、平台資料與商業數字。公開作品集要先清理。 The diaries are rich, but they include internal links, names, emails, platform details, and business numbers. Clean them before public use.

必做

匿名化

把人名改成職稱；所有電子郵件、電話、SharePoint、Calendly、內部平台網址都不要公開。 Replace names with roles. Do not publish emails, phone numbers, SharePoint links, Calendly links, or internal-platform URLs.

必查

50 萬美元與平台退場因果 $500K and platform-sunset causality

可以說「原型幫助建立替代路徑」，但若要說「觸發退場」或「節省 50 萬美元」，需要文件或當事人確認。 You can say the prototype helped create a replacement path. Claims like "triggered sunset" or "saved $500K" need confirmation.

建議

避免 AI 過度包裝

2019 年沒有 AI 實作。2026 年的說法應該是「配對系統、分類語彙、資料品質」，再延伸到 AI 時代仍然相關。 There was no AI implementation in 2019. The 2026 framing should be matching system, taxonomy, and data quality, then extend to AI-era relevance.
