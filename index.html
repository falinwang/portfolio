Why I design

I pay close attention to the gap between what users say and what they actually do.
That tension is where the real design problem usually lives — and why I question every brief before touching a pixel.

01

Reframe before designing

The most expensive mistake in product design is solving the wrong problem beautifully. I question the brief before touching a pixel.

02

Judgment over iteration

I track the fork in the road, not the round count. The decision that mattered most tells you more than how many versions were explored.

03

Influence over deliverables

I measure success by who changed their mind. A design that rewrites the PRD is worth more than a polished handoff deck.
